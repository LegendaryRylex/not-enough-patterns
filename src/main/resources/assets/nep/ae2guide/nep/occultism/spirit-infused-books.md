<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Binding One</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

There are eight books: a crusher and a crystallizer for each of the foliot, djinni, afrit and marid. Each has its own ritual.

* The ritual runs on Occultism's **crafting** pentacle for that spirit's tier, not its summoning pentacle.
* It is started with a bound Book of Binding for that tier, which the ritual consumes.
* The bowls hold the same offerings Occultism's own ritual for summoning that spirit asks for, plus one empty Book of Binding.
* A book is a one-time cost: it is never used up inside the Matrix.

The recipe viewer shows every one of the eight rituals in full.

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Using One</Color>
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

* The cover keeps the colour of its spirit's Book of Binding, and the mark on it shows the job: an orange hammer for a crusher, a blue crystal for a crystallizer. The tooltip names both.
* Shift-clicking a book in the Matrix screen puts it straight into the slot for its job; a crusher never fits the crystallizer slot, or the other way round.
* Breaking the Matrix drops its books along with everything else it holds.
* In a Ritual Controller only the tier counts, so a book of either job works there.
